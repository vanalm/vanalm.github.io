How it works
  • Each bar represent the amount of water in an individual pot (ml).
  • Consumption is calculated every 0.25 hr using the integral of the transpiration curve, scaled by each plant’s random “uniformity factor” (Normal(1, σ)) (this accounts for differences in plant health, leave surface area, environment, etc).
  • Scale sample is a random subset of plants picked to represent the crop for steering irrigation.
  • Irrigation trigger: when the average of the Scale pots drops to/below the “Lower volume limit”, the whole crop is irrigated (+Per-Irrigation volume).
  • Seed makes the random plants and Scale selection repeatable.
  • As time passes, small plants get wetter, and big plants get dryer, causing a spiral effect, ultimately leading to significant decrease in yield. when pots are "flushed", they are irrigated until water runs out the bottom of the pots, flushing out hydroxide ions and ferlizer residues to recover optimal ph and water potential
