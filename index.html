About Me

I’m a software engineer, consultant, and entrepreneur specializing in building AI/ML applications and production infrastructure for early-stage startups. I lead 0→1 engineering using Agile methodologies, and I’m a named inventor on a filed provisional patent in AI system design.

Based on Maui, I’m driven by the art and science of bringing ideas into reality. I love the process of defining the aperture of focus on a project: starting with a high-level objective and translating it into clear system design, milestones, and execution.

CocoWireless

Founder — cocowireless.org

Founded CocoWireless, an early LLM-powered SMS chatbot providing surf conditions and weather updates. Built a custom RAG pipeline optimized for domain-specific retrieval and delivered via Twilio (SMS) and AWS Lambda. Supported 20+ active users in production.

CocoWireless is a surf and weather report tool that users can access through the native messaging app on any phone. It is an LLM based app that provides realtime information through notifications or chat. Version 1 was built on Heroku, V2 in the AWS ecosystem with a serverless architecture. I manage state and conversation history manually, and perform simple and effective RAG operations. Check it out at www.cocowireless.org.

Etxt.ai

Founder — etxt.ai

Built Etxt.ai, an SMS-based system for automating responses to business inquiries for small business owners. The system generates custom, easily editable draft responses that users can review and send directly from SMS.

Etxt.ai is a way for small growing business to get ahead of their competition by responding to inquiries to their business faster. This mvp was built and deployed to test the assumptions that:

  • There is trust in AI to compose and send emails with supervision
  • There is entrepreneurial pain caused by the need to be "first to respond" to emails
  • There are users that prefer interacting over text message rather than existing AI email assistants

Validated early demand through smoke testing with Google Ads, achieving approximately 1.5% click-through rate (CTR).

The MVP was deployed and reviewed with useful results, and a google ads campaign was carried out to smoketest the concept as well. Future development is pending time! Learn more at etxt.ai

Agent-Driven Development Practices

Developed and applied best practices for agent-driven development, including:

  • Designing agents as composable, observable systems rather than monolithic prompts
  • Establishing evaluation loops for reasoning quality, tool usage, and failure modes
  • Building memory systems that balance persistence, relevance, and cost

These practices have been applied across startups, nonprofits, and client projects.
